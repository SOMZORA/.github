## About SOMZORA

**SOMZORA Technologies** is a Somali-founded technology company focused on
engineering modern software, developing digital products, and applying
emerging technologies to solve real-world problems.

We build technology for businesses, institutions, and communities — with a
long-term ambition to create scalable products and platforms that serve
Somalia, Africa, and the global market.
